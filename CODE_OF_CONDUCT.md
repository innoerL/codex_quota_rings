# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Welcome contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary.

To report a conduct concern, contact a maintainer through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If none is available, request a private contact channel without including sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled with discretion; do not post another person's private information.
